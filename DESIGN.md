## Thesis

Yubrio is a quiet social signal that becomes vivid around real friend activity. It refuses the default dark feed of equal cards and gives the first useful action a spatial, memorable home.

## Own-world

Stickers and color blocks. Warm dark canvas, flat saturated activity colors as whole room blocks, solid outlines with hard offset shadows, emoji and avatars as die-cut stickers that sit slightly crooked, and the round Fredoka display face for anything that speaks loudly. Lime stays the Yeb color. A second, brighter Glass mode swaps the sticker edges for translucent panes over soft colored light; layout and type stay the same.

## Story

The user can answer three questions at a glance: is anyone around, what is happening, and what can I start? Empty states feel like a quiet room, not a failed feed.

## First viewport

The first viewport opens on your own status with a one-tap “I’m free”, then free friends as a row of faces, then a short list of open rooms. It is never an infinite stream.

## Form

Operate mode with spatial composition instead of stacked inventory. The home board is the expressive moment; detail screens become calmer and more useful. Motion is short and tactile: signals enter with spring, actions compress, and meaningful state changes get a restrained accent pulse. All core actions remain accessible without relying on gestures.

## Color and type

Dark (default): `#211D19` canvas, `#2E2924` cards, cream ink, black outline and hard shadows, solid activity fills. Glass: pale canvas under a pastel light backdrop, translucent white panes with a white rim and soft shadow, activity colors as tinted glass. Lime `#D4FF3A` is the Yeb color in both and always carries near-black text. Fredoka Bold for titles and buttons, system sans for body.
