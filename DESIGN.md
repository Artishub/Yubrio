## Thesis

Yubrio is a quiet social signal that becomes vivid around real friend activity. It refuses the default dark feed of equal cards and gives the first useful action a spatial, memorable home.

## Own-world

True ink-black surfaces, warm white type, acid-lime energy, and a small set of coral, violet, sky, and amber presence marks. Color is carried by compact signal tiles, activity icons, and intentional action accents; surfaces are mostly open space with occasional soft elevation. Glass is reserved for floating navigation and live context.

## Story

The user can answer three questions at a glance: is anyone around, what is happening, and what can I start? Empty states feel like a quiet room, not a failed feed.

## First viewport

The first viewport opens directly into a live signal board. “See who is available” is the headline, “Open a room” is the primary action, and “I’m free” is a quieter secondary action with a short explanation. Room moments follow as a horizontal deck, never an infinite stream.

## Form

Operate mode with spatial composition instead of stacked inventory. The home board is the expressive moment; detail screens become calmer and more useful. Motion is short and tactile: signals enter with spring, actions compress, and meaningful state changes get a restrained accent pulse. All core actions remain accessible without relying on gestures.

## Color and type

Dark mode leads for short mobile use at mixed indoor light: #090B0D canvas, #14191D surfaces, #F4F7F2 ink, #AAB3B5 muted text, and #D9FF57 lime. Activity colors stay semantic and sparse. The system sans keeps body text native and readable; hierarchy comes from scale, weight, and placement rather than all-caps labels.
