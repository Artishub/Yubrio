# UX principles

1. **Useful in seconds.** Home answers “who is free?” and “what can I do?” immediately.
2. **Low pressure by design.** Joining is immediate; no RSVP language, public counts, or embarrassment states.
3. **Calm default, lively change.** Reserve strong color, haptics, and motion for availability, joining, creation, and meaningful updates.
4. **Progressive disclosure.** Common rooms need activity, time, place, and audience. Advanced online details stay behind relevant choices.
5. **Mutual privacy.** Visibility and messageability are audience decisions, enforced by the backend.
6. **Context over inbox.** Messages belong to an active availability or room; there is no dedicated permanent Messages tab.
7. **Native predictability.** Use sheets for quick choices, push navigation for details, standard back behavior, and accessible controls.
8. **No engagement traps.** No feed, streaks, XP, rankings, follower metrics, or notification spam.
